Game Information

What is Pokémon Null?

Pokémon Null is a heavily modified ROM hack of Pokémon Emerald built from the ground up for the Nuzlocke experience. Every trainer has been hand-crafted, every route rebalanced, and the Pokédex expanded to include Pokémon from Generations 1–9 — including brand-new Mega Evolutions. Expect no mercy.

Download & Play Read the FAQ
Combat

275 Hand-Crafted Trainer Battles

Every single trainer in Pokémon Null has been personally designed with a competitive mindset. Enemies use synergistic held items, teams that work together, and a custom AI that plays like nothing you've seen in a ROM hack before. No more free wins.

  • Competitive movesets and held items on all trainers
  • Custom AI that works to counter your strategy
  • Gym leaders with full synergistic 6-Pokémon teams
  • Super-hard rematches with rivals and bosses
Nuzlocke

Built for the Nuzlocke Challenge

Pokémon Null was designed from day one with Hardcore Nuzlocke rules in mind. Every encounter, every item placement, and every trainer fight has been balanced around the assumption that you might lose a Pokémon — and that losing one should matter. And you will lose Pokémon. A lot of them.

  • Encounter tables tuned for Nuzlocke variety
  • Resource management to help you adjust your team to your liking
  • Catching Pokémon made easier - but not guaranteed
  • Meticulously balanced so that no single Pokémon is overpowered and all teams have a chance
Pokédex

Generations 1–9 + New Megas

The Pokédex has been expanded to include Pokémon from all nine generations, including the brand-new Mega Evolutions from Z-A. Typing and stats remain the same as you remember them, but updated and changed abilities keep the experience fresh and unpredictable.

  • Pokémon from Generations 1–9 available
  • New Mega Evolutions for beloved Pokémon
  • Updated abilities with new functionality
  • 50+ generational lines have simplified evolution methods so you can evolve all your favorites
Quality of Life

Modern Conveniences, Classic Feel

No grinding, no HM slaves, no frustration (from the menu, at least). Pokémon Null ships with a full suite of quality-of-life features so you can focus on what matters: building your team and surviving the next fight.

  • Portable PC, Toggled Repel, Heal party from the menu
  • Run anywhere, and fly to previously known locations with PokeRider
  • Easy access to both Mach and Acro bikes without needing to exchange them at the shop
  • Infinite Rare Candies and Candy Bag to instantly level you to the cap
  • Pre-damage and Pre-status made easy within the Party menu
