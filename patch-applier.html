How to Patch Pokémon Null

  1. Obtain a clean Pokémon Emerald ROM.
    We cannot provide ROMs. You must legally obtain your own copy of Pokémon Emerald(U)(TrashMan) in .gba format.
  2. Download the Pokémon Null patch file.
    You can get the latest patch from the Pokémon Null Discord server, or from this link.
  3. Go to the online patcher.
    Click the Apply Pokémon Null Patch button above to open the online patcher tool (RomPatcher.js).
  4. Upload your ROM and the patch file.
    In the patcher, select your clean Emerald ROM as the ROM file and the Pokémon Null .bps patch as the Patch file.
  5. Apply the patch.
    Click Apply patch. When finished, download the new, patched ROM.
  6. Play Pokémon Null!
    Load the patched ROM in your favorite GBA emulator and enjoy the adventure.
Having trouble?
  • Make sure your ROM is a clean, unmodified Pokémon Emerald(U)(TrashMan).gba file. We cannot and will not provide that here and you may not ask for it in the Discord.
  • Do not use a randomizer or previously patched ROM.
  • If you get errors, try a different browser or clear your cache.
  • For more help, join our Discord community.

Version 1.1

Balance

Move changes:
  • Baton Pass: 40 PP → 5 PP
  • Teleport: 20 PP → 5 PP
  • Flip Turn, Volt Switch, U-Turn, Parting Shot: 20 PP → 5 PP
  • Reversal: 15 PP → 5 PP
  • Wish, Strength Sap, Spiky Shield, Fake Out, Chilly Reception: 10 PP → 5 PP
  • Obstruct, Recycle: 10 PP → 1 PP
  • Baneful Bunker: 5 PP → 1 PP
Encounter changes:
  • Rockruff now holds a guaranteed Sun Stone
  • Morelull now holds a guaranteed Moon Stone
  • Slightly tweaked Game Corner
Fight changes:
  • Slightly adjusted Root Academy pools to decrease the amount of auto-lose Pokémon
AI fixes: Slight tweaks to unintentional behaviors, among others:
  • Moves with added effects (like Lunge) could still be considered as a status move if the attacker has Sheer Force
  • AI being able to use some status moves against a Pokémon with Purifying Salt
  • AI being able to switch when having any stats raised or when seeing KOs
  • Discharge AI mistaking Volt Absorb for Motor Drive
  • Trap AI trying to trap Ghost-types
  • AI seeing Retaliate boost delayed
Misc:
  • Route 104 Grandma now gives one Leppa Berry
  • Max Elixirs are now available in all Marts
  • Changed Leppa Berry yields from 20 to 5
  • Added a Sitrus tree in Route 123
  • Increased starting money

Fixes

  • Fixed Volt Switch effect happening on the target if the target activated its Berserk and consumed a Berry
  • Fixed Copycat and Mirror Move crashing if both Player and AI have these moves respectively
  • Fixed crit message being able to be displayed for fixed damage moves like Dragon Rage
  • Fixed Magic Guard Pokémon getting freed immediately from trapping moves
  • Fixed Salt Cure's strengthened effect against Water and Steel types
  • Fixed Encore locked Pokémon sometimes targeting themselves
  • Fixed Magic Guard not protecting from Powder damage
  • Fixed Alluring Voice not being Sheer Force boosted
  • Fixed Rocky Payload having the wrong multiplier
  • Fixed healing moves when hitting a Substitute
  • Fixed Slow Start not restoring Speed

Additional fixes / adjustments

  • Fixed wrong orders in Level up learnsets causing Pokémon to skip moves (Shellder, Nidoran line)
  • Fixed weird behaviors and crashes when putting Common Candy and Candy Jar in the select-list
  • Slightly adjusted Guitarist Shawn & Guitarist Kirk to prevent soloing with 1 Pokémon
  • Fixed Petalburg City Mart Water Stone disappearing once you progress the story
  • Fixed giving Pokémon abilities they don't have, creating various doc issues

  • Fixed Rustboro City Mart items disappearing once you progress the story
  • Fixed Moody not being updated to modern generation behavior

  • Fixed Snap Trap having the wrong BP (90 BP → 80 BP)
