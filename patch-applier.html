How to Patch Pokémon Null

  1. Obtain a clean Pokémon Emerald ROM.
    We cannot provide ROMs. You must legally obtain your own copy of Pokémon Emerald(U)(TrashMan) in .gba format.
  2. Download the Pokémon Null patch file.
    You can get the latest patch from the Pokémon Null Discord server, or from this link.
  3. Go to the online patcher.
    Click the Apply Pokémon Null Patch button above to open the online patcher tool (RomPatcher.js).
  4. Upload your ROM and the patch file.
    In the patcher, select your clean Emerald ROM as the ROM file and the Pokémon Null .bps patch as the Patch file.
  5. Apply the patch.
    Click Apply patch. When finished, download the new, patched ROM.
  6. Play Pokémon Null!
    Load the patched ROM in your favorite GBA emulator and enjoy the adventure.
Having trouble?
  • Make sure your ROM is a clean, unmodified Pokémon Emerald(U)(TrashMan).gba file. We cannot and will not provide that here and you may not ask for it in the Discord.
  • Do not use a randomizer or previously patched ROM.
  • If you get errors, try a different browser or clear your cache.
  • For more help, join our Discord community.
Patch Notes
Version 1.2

Overall Changes

  • Adjusted several learnsets. These changes include Pokémon learning moves at an earlier/later level, some moves being added/removed from certain Pokémon and some moves only being available by using a Heart Scale.
  • Adjusted several trainers. These changes include level adjustments, held item adjustments, moveset adjustments, added/replaced Pokémon on certain teams and previously separate trainer fights becoming consecutive. These adjustments affect fights from Wattson Split to League Split.
  • Adjusted certain abilities. These changes include Pokémon losing one of their possible abilities, some abilities being replaced by other abilities and AI gaining access to several new Hidden Abilities.
  • Adjusted encounter tables.
  • Added one new Trainer battle (Old Couple John & Jay) in Steven Split.
  • AI Pokémon now have maxed PP in all moves. Moves with 1 base PP for the player default to 8 PP for the AI (except Revival Blessing).
  • Candy Jar and Common Candy can no longer be registered.

Due to the sheer amount of changes, specific cases are not introduced here. For updated information, please refer to the game's documentation.

Move-Related Changes

Effect Changes
  • Obstruct effect: −2 Defense → −1 Defense
  • Tera Starstorm: now has a 50% chance of either paralyzing, freezing, or burning the target.
Accuracy Changes
  • Zap Cannon, Inferno, Dynamic Punch: 50 Acc → 60 Acc
  • Wildbolt Storm, Bleakwind Storm, Springtide Storm, Sandsear Storm: 100 Acc → 95 Acc
  • Sizzly Slide, Buzzy Buzz: 90 Acc → 95 Acc
Base Power Changes
  • Attack Order, Rock Climb: 90 BP → 80 BP
  • Psystrike: 100 BP → 95 BP, 10 PP → 5 PP
  • Aeroblast: 100 BP → 95 BP
  • Water Shuriken: 15 BP → 20 BP, now has +2 priority, but is no longer guaranteed to hit 5 times when used by Greninja.
PP Changes
  • Photon Geyser: 5 PP → 10 PP
  • Last Respects, Life Dew, Chilly Reception: 10 PP → 5 PP
  • Spotlight: 15 PP → 5 PP
  • Follow Me, Rage Powder: 20 PP → 5 PP
  • Baton Pass: 40 PP → 5 PP
  • Burn Up: 5 PP → 1 PP
Tutor & TM Changes
  • Removed the Focus Energy Tutor from Petalburg City.
  • Added a Blizzard Tutor on Route 119.
  • Removed the Grass Knot TM from the Lilycove Department Store.
Other Move Property Changes
  • Hurricane: no longer considered a wind move.
  • Shadow Claw: now classified as a slicing move.
  • Tera Shift: now transforms into Stellar form if level 80 or above.
  • Trace: can no longer trace abilities which set terrain or weather.

Item-Related Changes

  • All resist berries now yield 1.
  • PP Up no longer available in Meteor Falls.
  • Tyranitarite location changed to Sky Pillar.
  • Swapped the locations of Mental Herb and Metronome: Mental Herb can be obtained in Route 117, Metronome in Route 120.
  • Scanner item (Choice Band, Choice Scarf, Choice Specs and Covert Cloak) trades are now limited to a maximum of 10 trades.
  • Assault Vest, Leftovers, Life Orb and Rocky Helmet can no longer be held simultaneously, but can be traded between via a system similar to Scanner trades. The aforementioned trade restriction does not apply to this item pool.

AI Changes

Behavioral Improvements
  • Adjusted mid-turn Switch AI to account for PP stalling strategies. The AI will now correctly identify being out of PP. Additional AI checks have been added to recognize possible attempted PP stalls, to which the AI will react accordingly.
  • Additional conditions have been added to the existing Switch AI logic to improve the predictability.
AI Bug Fixes
  • Fixed AI attempting to use No Retreat multiple times.
  • Fixed AI refusing to use First Impression against Pokémon with Inner Focus, Shield Dust, or Covert Cloak.
  • Fixed AI refusing to use Electro Shot in rain.
  • Fixed AI not correctly calculating Retaliate in post-KO switch scenarios.
  • Fixed AI being incorrectly affected by Illusion in unintended cases.
  • Fixed various other undocumented but unintended AI behaviors, which will not be specified at this moment.

Bug Fixes

Battle Mechanics
  • Fixed issue causing items to disappear (primarily Mega Stones).
  • Fixed Teleport, Shed Tail, and similar moves causing the game to crash when the opponent faints.
  • Fixed Substitute graphics not disappearing after using pivot moves (e.g. U-Turn).
  • Fixed Defend Order incorrectly behaving like Protect.
  • Fixed Mortal Spin missing its contact property.
  • Fixed persistent ability mismatches between documentation, calculator and the game.
  • Fixed incorrect interactions between pivot moves (e.g. U-Turn) and Eject Button.
  • Fixed incorrect berry activation timing when affected by Leech Seed or Yawn.
  • Fixed Big Root not interacting correctly with Leech Seed.
  • Fixed Sheer Force affected moves bypassing abilities such as Static.
  • Fixed Sawsbuck's alternate sprites not loading the second frame correctly.
  • Fixed various Unnerve-related issues.
  • Fixed Toxic counter occasionally increasing more than intended.
  • Fixed incorrect Hard Press calculations.
  • Fixed Mirror Move failing when the previous move failed.
  • Fixed Mist duration inconsistencies.
  • Fixed hazard damage (e.g. Stealth Rock) being applied twice when an opposing White Herb Pokémon is affected by Intimidate.
  • Fixed Thermal Exchange incorrectly blocking Yawn.
  • Fixed incorrect PP values for Collision Course and Electro Drift.
  • Fixed incorrect weight values for some modern generation Pokémon.
  • Fixed Zen Mode activating on lead when HP conditions are met.
  • Fixed multiple issues related to Take Heart.
Overworld & Map
  • Fixed incorrect level ranges for Happiny in Artisan Cave and Terrakion in Southern Island.
  • Fixed Ninja Boy Hideo battle not being in rain.
  • Fixed Psychic Kayla reactivating Gauntlet Mode in Mt. Pyre upon interaction.
  • Fixed missing encounter tiles on Sky Pillar 2F.

Other

  • Updated Zinnia's theme to a more polished version (credits to IAGeezus).
  • Fixed Alakazite's location to its intended placement in Mossdeep Gym.
  • Improved Mr. Briney's script so he remains available at all locations.
  • Added evolution stones to Mauville City and Verdanturf Town marts to account for the post-Wattson lockdown.
  • Fixed Slowking-Galar's icon palette.
  • Frostbite Status is now healed after battle.
  • Added a Fly location to Mt. Chimney.
  • Floette-Eternal trades now only accept non-red Florges forms, which remains exclusive to the Game Corner.
  • All Swablus and Altarias are now female.
